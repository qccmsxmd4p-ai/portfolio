PFE Intern — Telecom Network & Supervision Engineer

03/2026 – 07/2026
Société Nationale de Radiodiffusion et de Télévision (SNRT)
Rabat, Morocco

Intelligent supervision of broadcast equipment, focused on corrective and predictive maintenance.

  • Designed and deployed an intelligent network supervision architecture for the broadcast chain, combining GNS3, Zabbix and SNMP.
  • Modeled a multi-VLAN network topology (UPS units, RFE, iGATEL, RDS/OMNIA) representing critical broadcast equipment in a GNS3 simulation environment.

PFA Intern — Phased Array Antenna

07/2025 – 08/2025
Société Nationale de Radiodiffusion et de Télévision (SNRT)
Rabat, Morocco

Design and simulation of a phased-array antenna network for electronic beam scanning.

  • Modeled and analyzed a Uniform Linear Array to study beamforming and electronic beam-scanning principles.
  • Developed the array factor mathematical model and progressive phase law to steer the beam in different directions.
  • Ran a Python parametric study on how element count, spacing and phase shift affect directivity, beamwidth and sidelobes.
  • Simulated a microstrip patch antenna array in HFSS, analyzing 2D/3D radiation patterns, gain, directivity and sidelobe level (SLL).
  • Studied amplitude-weighting techniques (Hamming, Chebyshev) to reduce sidelobes and optimize performance across scan angles.
