Telecom Network & Supervision Engineer — Final-Year Project (PFE)

03/2026 – 07/2026
Société Nationale de Radiodiffusion et de Télévision (SNRT)
Rabat, Morocco

Designed an intelligent supervision solution for an audiovisual broadcast chain, moving maintenance from reactive to predictive.

  • Designed and deployed an end-to-end supervision architecture for the broadcast chain using GNS3, Zabbix and SNMP.
  • Modeled a multi-VLAN topology (UPS units, RFE, iGATEL, RDS/OMNIA) in GNS3 to reproduce critical broadcast equipment in a simulated environment.
  • Set up a Zabbix monitoring platform with SNMP metric collection, host and trigger configuration, and real-time alerting.
  • Queried and configured broadcast equipment through its vendor MIB (TREDESS-FS-MIB) with SNMPGET/SNMPSET to track device status and critical parameters.
  • Identified the key indicators needed to anticipate equipment failures, supporting a preventive maintenance approach.

RF & Antenna Engineering Intern — Phased Array (PFA)

07/2025 – 08/2025
Société Nationale de Radiodiffusion et de Télévision (SNRT)
Rabat, Morocco

Designed and simulated a phased-array antenna for electronic beam steering.

  • Modeled and analyzed a Uniform Linear Array to master beamforming and electronic beam-steering principles.
  • Derived the array factor model and the progressive phase law used to steer the beam toward different directions.
  • Built a Python parametric study of how element count, spacing and phase shift affect directivity, beamwidth and grating lobes.
  • Simulated a microstrip patch antenna array in HFSS and evaluated 2D/3D radiation patterns, gain, directivity and sidelobe level (SLL).
  • Applied amplitude-tapering techniques (Hamming, Chebyshev) to reduce sidelobes and optimize array performance across scan angles.
